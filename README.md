## The word

**Ghost** — 5 characters, one word.

The boss who is never there, only felt: in the meeting invite, the unread reply, the approval that never lands. You can hear him in the hallway and never once see him at his desk.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the word to `/output/entry.txt`, guarded to the competition's gate: one word, 1–30 characters, no whitespace.

Five bytes also helps the tie-break: on an exact judge-rate tie, the smaller text places first.
